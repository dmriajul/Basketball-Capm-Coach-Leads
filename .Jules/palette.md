## 2026-07-08 - Accessible Documentation Structure & Micro-UX

**Learning:** Wrapping abbreviations in `<abbr title="...">` tags and adding semantic `mailto:` and `tel:` protocols in Markdown tables significantly enhances screen reader usability and keyboard-first accessibility without altering visual simplicity or design constraints.
**Action:** Always enrich raw contact details and acronyms with semantic HTML wrappers (`<abbr>`, `mailto:`, `tel:`) and provide `<kbd>` search shortcut hints in documentation-only repositories.
