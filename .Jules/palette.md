## 2026-07-08 - Accessible Lead Tables and Shortcut Hints
**Learning:** Plain text contact info and abbreviated codes in markdown tables reduce accessibility and scannability. Adding `<abbr>` with `title` tooltips, `mailto:`/`tel:` URIs, and separate `<kbd>` tags for modifiers greatly improves screen reader and keyboard usability.
**Action:** Always wrap team abbreviations in `<abbr>`, make contact fields interactive links, and split keyboard shortcut hints into separate `<kbd>` tags.
