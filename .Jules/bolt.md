## 2026-07-08 - Data Access Optimization in Documentation Repositories

**Learning:** In documentation-only repositories, 'Time-to-Action' (TTA) and 'Time-to-Content' (TTC) are the core performance metrics. Providing lightweight inline preview tables and direct CSV/pubhtml download links reduces initial payload size from ~325KB (Google Sheets editor) to ~1.7KB (~99% reduction).

**Action:** Inline top leads with structured abbreviations (<abbr>), direct outreach URIs (mailto:, tel:), and search hints (<kbd>) to maximize data access speed and user efficiency.
