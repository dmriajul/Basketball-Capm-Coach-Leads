# Palette's Journal - Critical UX/Accessibility Learnings

## 2026-03-18 - High-Impact Typo Correction
**Learning:** In documentation-centric repositories, the primary header is the "hero" of the UI. A typo in the main title (e.g., "Capm" instead of "Camp") significantly degrades professional trust and searchability. Correcting it is a foundational micro-UX improvement.
**Action:** Always verify the spelling and formatting of the primary project header before proceeding with secondary UX enhancements.
