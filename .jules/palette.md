## 2026-05-01 - UX Patterns for Documentation-Centric Repositories
**Learning:** In documentation-only projects where the README acts as the primary UI, prioritize horizontal navigation bars with descriptive 'title' attributes and accessible acronym expansions (<abbr>) to reduce 'Time-to-Content' and improve screen reader context.
**Action:** Incorporate accessible acronyms, thematic icons, and structured navigation in all documentation-first repositories.
