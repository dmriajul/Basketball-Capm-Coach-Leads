## 2026-07-08 - Accessible Contacts and Shortcuts
**Learning:** Adding semantic abbreviations (`<abbr>`) for team codes, accessible non-protocol labels for contact links (`mailto:` and `tel:`), and GitHub-native blockquotes with nested keyboard tags improves data readability and Time-to-Action for users scanning lead tables.
**Action:** Always wrap team abbreviations in `<abbr>` with verbose title text, use clean numeric labels for phone links, and ensure keyboard shortcuts use distinct `<kbd>` tags inside fully blockquoted `[!TIP]` admonitions.
