## 2026-06-08 - Documentation-as-UI Initial Enhancement
**Learning:** In documentation-only repositories, the README.md serves as the primary User Interface. Reducing 'Time-to-Action' (TTA) and 'Time-to-Content' (TTC) through semantic HTML (<abbr>, <kbd>) and emoji-prefixed contact links significantly improves accessibility and utility.
**Action:** Prioritize scannable tables with direct action links and visual cues in future documentation-heavy projects.
