## 2026-03-20 - Enforcing Persona Boundaries for Micro-UX

**Learning:** When tasked with a "micro-UX improvement" (max 50 lines), avoid the temptation to build out missing infrastructure or performance-oriented features (like data cleaning scripts or extensive previews) that belong to other personas.

**Action:** Focus strictly on semantic HTML, accessibility (like `<abbr>` tags for domain-specific acronyms), and fixing professionalism-impacting typos in the primary interface (README). Keep all changes tightly scoped and verifiable.
