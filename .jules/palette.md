## 2026-03-31 - [Acronym Accessibility & Search Hints]
**Learning:** For documentation repositories, micro-UX wins like `<abbr>` tags for acronyms and `<kbd>` hints for native search (Ctrl+F) significantly lower the barrier to information discovery without bloating the codebase.
**Action:** Prioritize accessibility tooltips and platform-agnostic keyboard hints in README-first projects.
