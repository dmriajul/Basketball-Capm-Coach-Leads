## 2026-04-07 - [Horizontal Navigation in Documentation-only Repos]
**Learning:** In repositories where the README.md is the primary UI, implementing a horizontal navigation bar with descriptive `title` attributes significantly reduces 'Time-to-Action' and improves accessibility for users seeking specific sections.
**Action:** Use a compact, inline list of anchor links with `title` tooltips at the top of long README files to enhance scannability and professional polish.
