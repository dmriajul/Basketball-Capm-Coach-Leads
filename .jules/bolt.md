## 2026-05-01 - Optimizing Documentation-Centric Repositories
**Learning:** In repositories where the README.md acts as the primary UI, Time-to-Content (TTC) and Time-to-Action (TTA) are the most critical performance metrics. Payload reduction via 'pubhtml' exports and zero-latency data previews are highly effective.
**Action:** Always prioritize inlining high-value data segments and providing low-payload 'Fast View' links to external resources.
