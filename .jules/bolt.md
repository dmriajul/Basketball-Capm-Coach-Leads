## 2026-05-31 - Documentation as a UI Performance
**Learning:** In documentation-only repositories, the README.md is the primary interface. Performance is measured by Time-to-Content (TTC) and Time-to-Action (TTA). Providing a direct 'pubhtml' export link reduces data payload by ~97% (9KB vs 300KB+) compared to the standard Google Sheets editor.
**Action:** Prioritize 'Fast View' links and inlined semantic previews with <abbr> and emoji-prefixed contact protocols to optimize TTA.
