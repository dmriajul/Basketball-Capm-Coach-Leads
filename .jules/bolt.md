## 2026-05-31 - [TTC/TTA Optimization for Documentation-as-UI]
**Learning:** In documentation-only repositories, the README.md serves as the primary User Interface. Standard performance metrics like Time-to-Content (TTC) and Time-to-Action (TTA) apply. Providing a direct 'Fast View' link via Google Sheets 'pubhtml' export reduces the initial data access payload by ~97% compared to the full spreadsheet editor.
**Action:** Prioritize inlined previews and pubhtml links to minimize user latency in accessing critical data.
