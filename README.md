# 🏀 Basketball-Camp-Coach-Leads

![Last Updated](https://img.shields.io/badge/last%20updated-April%202026-blue.svg)

A repository for managing and organizing basketball coaching leads across various levels.

## 🔍 Quick Find
To quickly search for specific teams, coaches, or levels within the dataset, use:
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> (or <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> on Mac)

## 📚 Reference & Glossary
To assist with navigation and accessibility, here are the common acronyms used in this project:

- **<abbr title="National Basketball Association">NBA</abbr>**: Professional basketball league in North America.
- **<abbr title="National Collegiate Athletic Association">NCAA</abbr>**: Non-profit organization that regulates student athletes from over 1,100 North American institutions.
- **<abbr title="Division I">DI</abbr>**: The highest level of intercollegiate athletics sanctioned by the NCAA.
- **<abbr title="Division II">DII</abbr>**: An intermediate level of competition at the collegiate level.
- **<abbr title="Division III">DIII</abbr>**: The level of collegiate athletics where no athletic scholarships are offered.
